Our AI-assisted incident management platform simplifies your incident response process by providing all the tools you need in one place so you can wade into the fray with confidence.

Incident Management

Create, document, track, close, and reopen incidents, all while real-time chat allows your team to collaborate and share information with ease. Everything is documented for review and audit, including who participated, what systems were affected, and more. 

Resource Catalog

Track resources like servers, load balancers, network switches, VPC’s, virtual machines, applications, and more, each with their own ownership, location, and custom metadata. Attach resources to incidents to generate metrics for analysis and review. 

Root Cause Analysis

Dive deep into your incidents to find issues, set remediation agendas, and uncover areas that need improvement. Share RCA docs internally and externally in a variety of formats. 

Metrics & Analytics

Uncover hidden patterns, understand your infrastructure better, and look for key areas for improvement. Track common metrics like MTTA and MTTR, identify problem resources, and much more.
